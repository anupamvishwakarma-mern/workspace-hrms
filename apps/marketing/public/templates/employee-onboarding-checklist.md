# Employee Onboarding Checklist (Template)

## Before day one
- [ ] Signed offer and agreement received
- [ ] Employee record created and invitation sent
- [ ] Equipment and accounts prepared
- [ ] Manager and buddy assigned

## Day one
- [ ] Welcome and introductions
- [ ] Policies shared (attendance, leave, conduct)
- [ ] Mobile app installed and first check-in done
- [ ] Documents uploaded (identity, qualifications)

## First week
- [ ] Shift and work location confirmed
- [ ] Leave balance explained
- [ ] Goals for the first 30 days agreed

## First month
- [ ] Check-in conversation with manager
- [ ] Feedback collected from the new joiner
